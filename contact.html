Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and process.

What services does RdDev offer?

RdDev offers a wide range of software development services including web development, mobile app development, custom software solutions, database management, API development, and IT consulting.

How much does a typical project cost?

Project costs vary significantly based on scope, complexity, and requirements. We provide custom quotes for each project after an initial consultation to understand your specific needs.

How long does the development process take?

Development timelines depend on project complexity. Simple websites may take a few weeks, while complex enterprise applications could take several months. We provide timeline estimates during the planning phase.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes, we offer various support and maintenance packages to ensure your software continues to run smoothly after launch. This includes updates, bug fixes, and technical support.
